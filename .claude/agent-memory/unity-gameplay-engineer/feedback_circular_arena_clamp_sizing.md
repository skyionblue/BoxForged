Learned 2026-08-25 fixing B87 in `CulDeSac_WildWestCity` (see [[project_wildwestcity_build]] for the full scene-specific writeup and exact values). Generalizes to any future scene needing a `PlayerController` arena-clamp override.

**Never assume `_arenaCenter` should stay at a script default or a "plausible" pivot (world origin, a parent's transform, etc.) — always re-derive it from the live `Ground`/`NavMesh` geometry.** In this case the task itself suggested world origin as the center (reasoning that a whole-scene rotation preserves radius-from-origin) — that reasoning was correct but the premise was stale: the `Ground` plane's own transform position had drifted off world origin during an earlier, unrelated resize/re-cluster pass. Always read `Ground`'s actual `Renderer.bounds`/transform position live and cross-check against `NavMesh.CalculateTriangulation()`'s vertex AABB center (they should match closely) before picking a center.

**A rectangle's true "safe radius" is not its half-diagonal or its AABB corner distance — it varies sharply by angle, and a single circle cannot fit a non-square rectangle well.** Map it empirically: raycast down onto the `Ground` `MeshCollider` from well above (e.g. y=100, not y=5 — starting low enough to be *inside* a building's roof mesh gives false "void" reads since the ray never exits the mesh to register a hit) at fine angular steps (10°) and radial steps (0.25-0.5m) from the candidate center, walking outward until the first "no hit at all" (true void) — distinguish this from "hit a building" (still solid, not a real problem) by checking the hit collider, not just hit/no-hit. This gives the real per-angle edge profile. For a rectangle centered at its own centroid, the tightest safe angle equals `min(halfWidth, halfLength)` (here: ~20m flank-perpendicular vs ~24m along-length) — provable and matches the empirical sweep almost exactly.

**When the required destinations (e.g. spawn, "the far end of the street") sit closer to the long-axis extent than the short-axis safe limit, there is no radius that is simultaneously fully safe in every direction — pick a radius that satisfies the required walk-paths and document the residual risk in directions nothing requires reaching**, rather than either (a) picking a radius so small it clamps the player right at spawn (a real, worse regression — "premature clamping" at t=0), or (b) picking a radius large enough to reach every literal corner, which would leave much bigger unprotected void gaps elsewhere. Cross-check candidate destinations against `NavMesh.CalculateTriangulation()` vertices (guaranteed-walkable) and `NavMesh.CalculatePath(...).status == PathComplete` to confirm they're real, reachable points, not isolated pockets.

**Verify the final chosen values by directly invoking the private clamp method via reflection on teleported test points in Play Mode**, rather than trusting the geometry math alone — e.g. `pc.GetType().GetMethod("ClampToArenaBounds", NonPublic|Instance).Invoke(pc, null)` after setting `transform.position`, then check `moved == 0` at real destinations and correct clamp-to-exact-radius at a deliberately out-of-bounds point. This is fast, deterministic, and doesn't depend on simulating real input or waiting on Play Mode's unreliable frame-advance-between-tool-calls behavior (see [[feedback_rigid_rotation_technique]] point 5).

**Setting the values as a prefab-instance override (not touching the shared prefab asset) is done via `SerializedObject` + `ApplyModifiedProperties()` on the scene-instance component** — this creates the correct minimal `PrefabInstance.m_Modifications` entries in the scene file (verified: exactly the touched property paths, e.g. `_arenaCenter.x`, `_arenaCenter.z`, `_arenaBoundaryRadius`, nothing else) while leaving the source `.prefab` asset with zero `git diff`. Confirm both sides explicitly: read the live instance's `SerializedObject` after the edit, and separately load the prefab asset via `AssetDatabase.LoadAssetAtPath` and read its `SerializedObject` to prove it's unchanged (or just check `git status` shows no entry for the asset file at all).
