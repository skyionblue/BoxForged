Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

We don't collect your data.
Simple as that.

Overview

BoxForged is a mobile action game developed by SkyionBlue. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect — and the answer is: none.

Information We Collect

BoxForged does not require an account, does not connect to the internet during gameplay, and does not transmit any data from your device to us or to any third party.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage data, analytics, or crash reports sent to us
  • Any data entered into the game

Third-Party Services

BoxForged does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data collection services inside the app.

Apple may collect certain diagnostic and usage data through TestFlight or the App Store as part of their standard platform services. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

BoxForged does not collect data from any users, including children under the age of 13. The game is safe for players of all ages.

Data Storage

Because we collect no data, there is nothing stored on our servers. All game data — settings and progress — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy in the future, we will post the revised version at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. We will notify TestFlight and App Store users of any material changes.
