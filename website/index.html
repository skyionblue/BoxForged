Choose Your Style.

Two fighting styles. Four heroes. The cardboard box you choose changes everything — your moves, your special ability, and how you take on The Unimaginative.

Fast, precise, and untouchable when the dodge connects. Dash through enemies and disappear into the dark.

Shadow Style

Tumble Style

The Unimaginative

They want your cardboard.

After The Great Hush, the World Tree became the last source of raw cardboard. The Unimaginative seized it — because they can't build anything themselves. They want to trade the ability to make for the comfort of consuming. Stop them.
