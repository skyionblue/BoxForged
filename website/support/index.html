Support

Having a problem with BoxForged, or found a bug?
We want to hear about it.

boxforged@gmail.com

What to include

The more detail you give us, the faster we can track it down:

  • What device and OS version you're playing on (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 17)
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

Bugs, crashes, and gameplay issues

Email us at the address above with what happened. We're a two-person team building this game live — we read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Feedback and ideas

BoxForged is built in public — the audience submits ideas, and the winning submission gets designed and built live on stream. If you've got a weapon, enemy, or story idea, drop it in the queue on Twitch rather than email, so it can go to a live vote.

Privacy questions

For questions about what data BoxForged collects, see our Privacy Policy.
