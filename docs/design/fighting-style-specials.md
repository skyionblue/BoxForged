# BoxForged — Fighting Style Specials

## How Specials Work

Each Fighting Style has a **Special Move** tied to the Special button (the star button in the HUD).

The **charge bar** (lightning bolt at the bottom-left of the HUD) fills over **8 seconds** after a special is fired. When the bar is full, the special is ready. The **Special button** on the action panel also dims while charging and brightens when ready.

A special can also be fired immediately from the **Counter Window** — the brief window that opens after a successful parry, before the bar is full.

---

## Ninja Style — Shadow Dash

**How to trigger:** Press Special when the charge bar is full, or immediately after a successful parry.

**What happens:**
- The player dashes **5 units forward** (in the direction they're facing) in 0.25 seconds
- During the dash the player's body turns **flat black** — a shadow silhouette
- Any enemy in the path of the dash takes **20 damage**
- Multiple enemies can be hit if they're lined up along the dash direction

**Passive bonus:** Dodge rolls grant **full invincibility frames** — the player cannot be damaged during a dodge.

**Best use:** Dash through a cluster of enemies, or use it after a parry to punish an enemy at close range.

---

## Cowboy Style — Tumbleshot

**How to trigger:** Press Special when the charge bar is full, or immediately after a successful parry.

**What happens:**
- Fires a **gold projectile** forward from the player's facing direction
- The bullet travels in a straight line at **20 units/sec** up to **15 units** of range
- The bullet **pierces the first enemy** it hits (continues traveling after dealing damage)
- The bullet **destroys on the second hit** or when it reaches max range

**Passive bonus:** Parry timing window is **wider (0.4 seconds)** instead of the default 0.3 seconds — more forgiving parry inputs.

**Best use:** Fire into a line of enemies to hit two targets with one shot. The wider parry window makes the Cowboy style more beginner-friendly for defense.

---

## Cooldown

Both specials share the same **8-second cooldown** before the charge bar refills. The cooldown begins the moment the special fires. There is no limit to how many times a special can be used per run — it recharges continuously.
