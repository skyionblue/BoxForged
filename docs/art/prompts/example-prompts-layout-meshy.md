## PIECE 4 — "She Doesn't Flinch" — Stills + Animation Cut

### Notes on this version

- Same 12-beat story and order as the previous version, matched to the finished art.
- Each shot now uses its corresponding finished still as the **starting frame** — upload it as the first-frame reference in Flow's image-to-video mode rather than generating the shot from text alone. This keeps every shot locked to the art you already approved instead of risking a fresh, possibly inconsistent generation.
- Three shots are marked **[STILL SHOT]**: the frame doesn't animate at all, consistent with how the quieter, dramatic beats in this franchise have always played (previous pieces routinely just "hold" and "fade" rather than keep moving). For these, you have two options: skip Flow entirely and drop the still directly into your edit timeline, or run it through Flow with motion strength set near zero for a very faint ambient drift. Both are noted per shot.
- The other nine are marked **[ANIMATED SHOT]** — the prompt describes the motion that continues out from the reference still's exact pose, not the whole scene from scratch.

**In-game cutscene. Duration: ~62 seconds. Shot count: 12. Aspect ratio: 16:9.**
