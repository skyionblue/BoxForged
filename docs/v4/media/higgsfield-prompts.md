# BoxForged — Higgsfield Video Prompts

**For:** Higgsfield AI video generation
**Purpose:** Zone trailers, character showcases, "What Kid Sees" transformation segments, App Store preview, social clips
**Game:** BoxForged V4 — a mobile action roguelite where Kid puts a cardboard box on her head and sees the world transformed by imagination
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05

---

## How to Use These Prompts

Each prompt is a self-contained Higgsfield input. Paste it directly into Higgsfield's text prompt field. Where noted, provide a reference image from the game (screenshot or render) to anchor character appearance.

**Higgsfield tips for this project:**
- Use **Image-to-Video** when you have a reference screenshot of the game scene — anchors the art style
- Use **Text-to-Video** for scenes with no in-game equivalent yet
- Set duration to **6–10 seconds** per clip (shorter = cleaner motion)
- Preferred motion: **slow and deliberate** — fast motion blurs the cardboard-marker aesthetic
- Aspect ratio: **16:9** for YouTube/App Store, **9:16** for TikTok/Reels

## Character Reference Sheet (provide to Higgsfield as image reference)

**Kid (Ninja box):**
A 10-year-old boy with chunky/stocky proportions. He wears a perfect cardboard cube on his head as a mask — dark navy blue with hand-drawn purple marker lines forming a ninja mask design. Eye cutouts are dark voids. He wears a dark blue hoodie and jeans. His proportions are deliberately exaggerated — the box is about 35% of his total height.

**Kid (Cowboy box):**
Same boy, same proportions. Box is leather brown cardboard with hand-drawn gold marker lines forming a cowboy hat brim on the sides. Worn like a helmet.

**Kid (Cowgirl box):**
Same proportions. Box is tan/cream cardboard with hand-drawn pink marker lines forming a bonnet/cowgirl hat shape.

**Art style anchor:**
Stylized 3D. Everything looks like it was made from cardboard, kraft paper, and marker pens. Surfaces have corrugated cardboard texture. Outlines are hand-drawn dark brown marker strokes, slightly uneven. Colors are vivid Crayola-marker quality — warm and bold, never neon. Chunky proportions throughout. Think Tearaway meets A Short Hike.

## SECTION 7 — General Style Notes for Higgsfield

### Always include in every prompt:
- "Stylized 3D, corrugated cardboard and kraft paper textures, hand-drawn dark brown marker outlines"
- "Chunky proportions — character head (box) is approximately 35% of total height"
- "Vivid Crayola-marker colors — bold and warm, never neon"
- "Art style reference: Tearaway (craft materials), A Short Hike (warm simplicity)"

### Camera motion guidelines:
- **Slow push-in or pull-back**: always works, generates clean motion
- **Static wide shots**: best for transformation reveals
- **Slow tracking** (following a walking character): keep walk speed deliberately slow
- **Avoid**: fast pans, rotations over 30° per second, handheld shake

### Character motion guidelines:
- Slow, deliberate actions read clearly
- Quick combat actions work in slow-motion (0.5x speed)
- The cardboard box should stay rigid on the character's head (not bounce around)
- Avoid complex multi-limb simultaneous motion

### What makes this art style unique:
The world looks like it was made by a child from craft supplies — but intentionally, proudly, with skill. The corrugated cardboard texture is always visible. The marker outlines are always present. Colors are vivid but warm. This is not trying to be realistic — it's trying to be the most beautifully handmade version of itself.

When in doubt: ask "does this look like it was made with cardboard and markers?" If yes, it's correct.
