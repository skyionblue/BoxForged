## Content Phases

### Phase 1 — Awareness (Now through App Store submission)

**Goal:** Get the idea in people's heads. Make them understand what this game is in one sentence. The premise — someone turned off the Internet, adults went grey, kids found cardboard boxes and didn't miss a step — is the hook. Say it plainly and repeatedly across every platform in different formats. The teaser video is the anchor for this phase; all Phase 1 posts should funnel toward it. Do not oversell the game state. We're building in public — the audience should feel like they're watching something real get made, not a polished PR campaign.

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts per week across all platforms combined. Minimum: one Build In Public post per Twitch stream. One piece of original content (post, reel, short) per week. Every sprint deliverable gets a post.

---

### Phase 2 — Engagement (After App Store submission through launch week)

**Goal:** Convert awareness into a community that cares about the outcome. This phase introduces characters, enemies, and zones one at a time. Community votes and polls become a bigger part of the content mix — things like "what should we name this enemy," "which weapon drops first," "poll: ninja or cowgirl for your main." The Build In Public mechanic becomes a story: we're this close, here's what's left, here's what broke this week. Every post should make someone feel like they have a stake in whether this launches.

**Cadence:** Daily posts across platforms. Pre-written post library (this doc) fills off-stream days. One Twitch stream per week minimum. Reels/Shorts for every major visual milestone.

---

### Phase 3 — Launch Day

**Goal:** Convert the community into Day 1 downloads. Launch day is a single coordinated moment: one post across all platforms at the same time, Twitch stream going live as the app goes live, pinned links to the App Store and Google Play on every profile. The window is 48 hours — everything in this phase happens on launch day and the day after. After that, content shifts to "here's what's coming next" to keep the Day 1 audience from churning.

**Cadence:** 3–5 posts on launch day itself (announcement, first screenshot from a player, thank-you, App Store link, evening recap). One stream. One YouTube short.

### 4. The Drained World and the Reclaimed World

```
Every zone in BoxForged exists in two states at the same time.

The Unimaginative version: grey, quiet, washed out.
A cracked cul-de-sac. Dead grass. Empty houses.
```

## Pre-Launch Checklist

Everything that must be in place before App Store and Google Play submission.

### Social Profiles

- [ ] Instagram handle confirmed (@unboxedheroes) — bio updated with website link and teaser link
- [ ] TikTok handle confirmed (@unboxedheroes) — bio updated
- [ ] Twitch profile updated — game title set, panels added (About, Schedule, Links)
- [ ] YouTube channel confirmed (@UnBoxedHeroes-c9q) — channel description updated, teaser pinned as channel trailer
- [ ] All profiles use the same profile image (logo or Kid character art — pick one and be consistent)
- [ ] All profiles link to theunboxedheroes.com

### Visual Assets

- [ ] App icon finalized (1024x1024 PNG, no rounded corners — App Store applies them)
- [ ] At least 6 App Store screenshots (iPhone and Android sizes) — in-game, not mockups
- [ ] App Store preview video (15–30 seconds, no audio required)
- [ ] Social profile banner / header image for each platform
- [ ] At least 1 gameplay GIF or short clip ready for launch day posts

### Content Bank

- [ ] Launch day announcement post written and scheduled for all platforms
- [ ] At least 3 posts queued for the 48 hours after launch (player reactions, "we did it", App Store link pinned)
- [ ] YouTube devlog covering the final sprint recorded and scheduled for launch week
- [ ] Twitch stream scheduled for launch day (stream as the app goes live)
- [ ] All evergreen posts from this doc adapted per platform and queued in a scheduling tool

### App Store and Google Play

- [ ] App Store description written (see media-kit.md for source copy)
- [ ] Google Play store listing written
- [ ] Age rating submitted and confirmed
- [ ] Privacy policy URL live (required for both stores)
- [ ] Support URL live (can be a contact form on theunboxedheroes.com)
- [ ] All App Store metadata reviewed for the name "BoxForged" — no legacy "BoxHead Ninjas" anywhere

### Outreach

- [ ] Sponsor and partner posts confirmed for launch week (see outreach docs in docs/media/)
- [ ] At least 3 indie game press / newsletter contacts emailed (not just DM'd)
- [ ] Community members who voted on stream features notified ahead of launch — they are the first wave
