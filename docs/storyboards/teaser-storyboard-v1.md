# Unboxed Heroes — Teaser Trailer Storyboard v1

**Format:** App Store / Website teaser — 60–90 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 landscape (mobile primary)
**Grid:** 20 panels / 4 columns / 5 rows
**Visual treatment:** Hand-labeled pre-vis boards. Pencil-rough aesthetic in generated references — finalized as cel-shaded 3D render with URP post-processing and color grade.
**Date:** 2026-07-24

---

## Narrative Brief

The teaser traces a single emotional arc: **grey to vivid.** It opens on the silence The Great Hush left behind — a world drained of screens, noise, and distraction. It follows one kid finding one cardboard box. From that moment, imagination is the only enemy of despair, and the only weapon against The Unimaginative who want to keep it that way. The trailer ends not with a "look how cool the game is" beat but with a quiet, earned answer: *your weird idea is the right idea.*

**Emotional targets (in order):** Recognition → Curiosity → Delight → Dread → Urgency → Triumph → Hope

### ROW 1 — The Great Hush

---

#### PANEL 01 — ESTABLISH / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "The Great Hush."

**Scene description:** Ultra-wide shot of a suburban cul-de-sac. It is neither ruined nor thriving — it is simply quiet. Houses intact. Trees alive. Cars in driveways. But every window is dark. No blue screen glow. No light from inside. Late afternoon, overcast sky. The whole frame is desaturated — a warm silver-grey, like a photograph left too long in a drawer. Three kids sit on a front stoop. They are not sad. They are simply... waiting, the way children wait when the thing that used to fill the time is gone.

**Motion/action notes:** Camera holds completely still. No wind. No ambient movement. A single leaf falls across the left third of the frame — the only motion for the full duration of this shot. Slow fade in from black opens the panel.

**Post-process:** Full desaturation. Slight blue-cool grade. Vignette at 40% opacity.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Suburban cul-de-sac street, late afternoon overcast light, low-poly stylized 3D, no screen light in windows, three children seated on front porch steps doing nothing, empty driveways, dead leaves on sidewalk, muted grey palette, wide establishing shot, horizon line at one-third, URP cel-shaded`

**Magnific AI style note:** Hand-drawn pencil sketch, crosshatch shadows, cool sepia wash, sparse linework — evokes a graphic novel establishing page. No color fills.

#### PANEL 02 — MEDIUM / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "No phones. No TV. No noise."

**Scene description:** Medium shot, waist up on two of the stoop kids. One holds a stick, tapping it against the step in a slow, rhythmic boredom pattern. The other stares at their own hand, turning it over like they have forgotten what to do with it. Behind them, through the dark window: a dead TV screen reflects only the sky. The frame composition places the window centrally in the background — it functions as a void, an absence the eye keeps returning to.

**Motion/action notes:** The stick-tapping is the only motion. Rhythm is slow — one tap every 1.5 seconds. The kid with the empty hand slowly closes their fist, then opens it again. Camera does not move.

**Post-process:** Full desaturation maintained. Background slightly more out of focus (shallow depth of field simulation via post).

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Two children sitting on porch steps, one tapping stick rhythmically, one looking at own open hand, dark window behind them showing dead TV screen reflecting sky, low-poly stylized 3D characters, muted grey-brown tones, medium shot waist-up, URP cel-shaded, no screen glow`

**Magnific AI style note:** Hand-drawn pencil sketch, warm sepia with cool shadow tones, children rendered simply with thick outlines, window as negative space.

#### PANEL 03 — CLOSE / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "Then."

**Scene description:** Extreme close-up on a pair of sneakers on pavement. The laces are undone on one shoe. Cracked sidewalk. A single ant moves across the concrete near the toe. The shot is deliberately mundane — almost abstract at this scale. Then: at the very edge of frame right, a cardboard box slides into view. Just the corner of it. Brown. Intact. Ordinary.

**Motion/action notes:** The box slides into frame from off-screen right — slow, as if pushed by a gentle wind or carried by a child just arriving. It stops when one corner is visible. Hold. The ant continues. Camera does not move. This is the first warm-toned object in the trailer.

**Post-process:** Desaturation at 90% — but the cardboard box is rendered at 60% desaturation, slightly warmer than everything else. The box is technically still grey, but it is the warmest grey in the frame. The viewer should feel the difference without being able to articulate it.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Extreme close-up of child's worn sneakers on cracked sidewalk, undone laces, single cardboard box corner entering frame from right edge, concrete texture, ant on pavement, low-poly stylized 3D, ground-level camera angle, warm brown cardboard against grey-blue pavement`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil sketch with ink wash, heavy concrete texture linework, cardboard rendered with slightly warmer hatching than surroundings — almost imperceptible warmth.

#### PANEL 04 — MEDIUM / PUSH IN

### ROW 2 — Imagination Ignites

---

#### PANEL 05 — CLOSE / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "What if."

**Scene description:** Close-up on the kid's face from slightly below — a subtle low-angle that makes them feel large against the washed-out sky. Their eyes are focused on something above frame (the top of the box). This is the first time we see their face fully. The expression is unmistakably a question forming into a decision. Not giddiness. Certainty. The kind of face a person makes right before they do something that will change things.

**Motion/action notes:** Static hold. The camera does not move. The only motion is the kid's eyes tracking across something imagined — left to right — as they see what the box could become. Blink. Then stillness. Duration: 2 seconds.

**Post-process:** The sky behind them gets a single degree warmer in color temperature — barely noticeable. Vignette tightens slightly, drawing the eye to the face.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Close-up low-angle portrait of 8-year-old child looking up with focused determined expression, sky background, no smile just certainty, low-poly stylized 3D, slightly below eye-level angle, overcast sky with subtle warmth beginning, URP cel-shaded, strong facial silhouette`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil and ink close-up portrait, Moebius-influenced clean linework on face, sky behind rendered as simple wash, expression carries the weight — no decoration.

#### PANEL 06 — CLOSE / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "The first fold."

**Scene description:** Close-up on the kid's hands folding a flap of the cardboard box. The fold is clean, deliberate — both thumbs pressing the crease in. This is not rough tearing. This is origami with conviction. The cardboard has a hand-drawn texture: you can see old marker stripes, a stamped shipping label, corrugated ridges. The warmth of the material fills the frame.

**Motion/action notes:** Single motion: both hands fold the flap down and crease it. The sound design note for this shot is critical — the creak and crinkle of cardboard is the only sound in this panel. It should feel huge in the silence of the Grey Hush world. Camera does not move.

**Post-process:** Desaturation lifts to 70% on the cardboard itself. Everything at the edge of frame remains grey. The cardboard is the only warm object. No bloom yet.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Extreme close-up of child's hands folding cardboard box flap, deliberate crease with both thumbs, corrugated cardboard texture visible, shipping label stamp, hand-drawn marker lines on cardboard surface, low-poly stylized 3D, warm brown tones, top-down slight angle on hands, URP cel-shaded`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil sketch with crosshatch cardboard texture, warm sepia tones on cardboard, hands outlined in clean ink, focus on the crease and the texture — material-forward frame.

#### PANEL 07 — MEDIUM / STATIC → SHAKE (single frame)
**Narrative caption:** "The box becomes the hat."

**Scene description:** Medium shot on the kid lifting the folded cardboard hat above their head with both hands. It is unmistakably a cowboy hat — wide brim, creased crown, a strip of rope threaded through the brim as a hatband. **Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Child lifting hand-folded cardboard cowboy hat above head with both arms outstretched, hat fully formed with wide brim and rope hatband, medium shot against grey suburban street, low-poly stylized 3D, hat warm brown fully saturated, child's clothing grey-muted, moment just before placing on head, URP cel-shaded`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil sketch, hat rendered in warm ink wash while background stays cool pencil grey — one warm object isolated in a grey world.

#### PANEL 08 — MEDIUM / PULL OUT
**Narrative caption:** "And then the other kid makes a mask."

**Scene description:** Pull back to reveal a second kid — slightly taller, same neighbourhood — standing five feet away. They have folded a small cardboard box into a ninja mask: rectangular, eye-slit cut with a key, marker lines drawn on the front. They hold the mask up in front of their face, not yet tied. **Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Two children standing in suburban cul-de-sac, one wearing cardboard cowboy hat, one holding cardboard ninja mask to face, both looking forward not at each other, grey muted street background, low-poly stylized 3D, two warm cardboard objects against grey world, medium wide shot, URP cel-shaded, late afternoon overcast`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil sketch, two warm ink-wash figures in a crosshatched grey environment — like two lit candles in a dark room.

### ROW 3 — Color Bleeds In

---

#### PANEL 09 — TOP-DOWN WIDE / STATIC
**Narrative caption:** "Color remembers how."

**Scene description:** Top-down wide shot (the gameplay camera perspective) — the full cul-de-sac seen from above. Both kids stand at center. From their feet, very slowly, a bloom of color begins to spread outward across the pavement: warm terracotta, grass green, sky blue creeping across the grey concrete in a radius that grows with each second. The color does not look like a visual effect — it looks like the world remembering what it was supposed to look like. Houses at the edge of frame are still grey.

**Motion/action notes:** Color bloom radiates from the exact center of the two kids' position. It moves slowly — about 1 meter per second in scale terms. By the end of the shot, roughly 30% of the visible area is reclaimed. This is the Imagination Restore effect from the game's zone-clear sequence, applied to the teaser.

**Post-process:** This is the trailer's first major post-process shift. Desaturation drops from 90% to 20% within the color bloom radius. Bloom intensity increases. Color grade shifts from cool-silver to warm-amber in the reclaimed area only. Vignette pulls back at the edges.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Top-down aerial view of suburban cul-de-sac, two small children at center, circular bloom of vivid color spreading from their feet across grey pavement, warm terracotta ground, green grass borders, blue sky reflection in puddle, grey houses at perimeter, low-poly stylized 3D, URP cel-shaded, dramatic color contrast between reclaimed center and grey edges`

**Magnific AI style note:** Top-down architectural sketch style, color bloom rendered as watercolor wash bleeding outward from center, grey areas remain pencil crosshatch — two visual languages colliding in one frame.

#### PANEL 10 — MEDIUM / FOLLOW
**Narrative caption:** "The backyard becomes a dojo."

**Scene description:** Camera follows the Ninja kid as they run — not sprint, but purposeful — into a backyard. As they run, the world transforms around them in the imagination overlay: the overgrown backyard becomes a feudal Japanese dojo. Training dummies appear made from fence posts and rope. **Motion/action notes:** Camera follows at a consistent distance — the same follow mechanics as the gameplay camera. The Ninja runs in a slight arc toward a training dummy. At the end of the shot: the Ninja spins and delivers one clean strike to the dummy. The dummy spins. Cut on impact.

**Post-process:** Color fully reclaimed within 3 meters of the Ninja character. Beyond that: partial desaturation creating a gradient halo of imagination. Motion blur at low intensity on the strike impact.

**Suggested Meshy AI prompt:** `Ninja child in cardboard mask running through backyard, imagination overlay transforming environment to feudal dojo, training dummies made from fence posts and rope, garden tools becoming weapons, warm wood-brown tones, low-poly stylized 3D, camera follow mid-shot, URP cel-shaded, motion trail on running`

**Magnific AI style note:** Pencil-and-ink kinetic sketch, speed lines on the Ninja's movement, dojo elements rendered in warm ink wash, grey real-world elements visible at frame edges — dual-layer aesthetic.

#### PANEL 12 — MEDIUM / STATIC

## Implementation Notes

### Timeline structure (Unity 6)

The trailer must be constructed as a Unity Timeline asset — no pre-rendered video. Each panel transition is a Timeline cut or blend.

| Timeline track type | Use |
|---|---|
| Cinemachine Shot track | Camera cuts and pushes (Panels 01, 04, 08, 09, 17, 18) |
| Animation track | Character motion (Panels 06, 07, 08, 10, 11, 15, 16, 17) |
| Post Processing track | Desaturation grade (all panels), color bloom trigger (Panels 09, 10, 18, 19, 20) |
| Activation track | Show/hide scene objects (storefront facade, dojo elements, scaffolding) |
| Audio track | Ambient silence, cardboard SFX, music sting triggers |
| Signal track | Fire Imagination Restore particle system at Panel 09 and Panel 18 |

### Color grade events (URP Volume system)

Three Volume profiles required:

1. **Vol_GreyWorld** — full desaturation (`Saturation = -100`), cool white balance (`Temperature = -15`), vignette intensity 0.4
2. **Vol_Transition** — partial desaturation (`Saturation = -40`), neutral temperature, bloom threshold 0.8
3. **Vol_Reclaimed** — no desaturation, warm white balance (`Temperature = +10`), bloom intensity 1.2, lens flare enabled

Blend between volumes using the Timeline Post Processing track with weight animation. Never switch volumes with a hard cut — blend over minimum 0.5 seconds.

### Cardboard bloom particle effect

Panels 09 and 18 require a circular particle system emitting from a point on the ground plane, expanding in a ring. Use URP Particle Shader (Unlit) with:
- Emission color: cardboard warm (`#B8925F`)
- Lifetime: 3 seconds
- Start size: 0.1, End size: 4.0
- Shape: Circle, Radius: expanding
- Render mode: Billboard

This system should be fired via a Signal in the Timeline at the exact frame the color bloom begins.

### Panels requiring camera animation

| Panel | Camera behavior | Cinemachine approach |
|---|---|---|
| 04 | Slow push in | Animate FOV from 60° to 52° over 2s on Cinemachine Virtual Camera |
| 08 | Pull out | Animate FOV from 52° to 65° over 2s |
| 13 | Tilt-down | Animate Cinemachine Camera Aim from sky Y=-30° to ground Y=+15° over 3s |
| 17 | Subtle shake | Cinemachine Noise component, Handheld_tele_mild preset, intensity 0.3 |
| 18 | Pull out | Animate Camera position Z on dolly track, from close to wide over 3s |

### Audio design cues

| Panel | Audio event |
|---|---|
| 01–04 | Ambient silence — no music, faint wind only |
| 06 | Cardboard crinkle SFX (priority mix — should feel enormous) |
| 07 | Hat-placement impact SFX — short, percussive, warm |
| 09 | Music enters — single piano note sustained, building slowly |
| 12 | Music dims — marching rhythm enters underneath |
| 17 | Music peaks — full arrangement with percussion and woodwinds |
| 18–19 | Music resolves to major key sustained chord |
| 20 | Silence for 1 second, then: soft logo sting |
