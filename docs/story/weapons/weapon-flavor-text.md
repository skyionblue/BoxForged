# Weapon Flavor Text

*Format: what it actually is / what the kid calls it.*

---

## Tier 1 — Fast

**Broomstick → Bo Staff**
*A broom with the bristles snapped off. Found it in the garage.*
*The Staff of the Morning Sweep. Strikes faster than thought.*

**Ruler → Shurikens**
*A twelve-inch wooden ruler, slightly warped. Says "Property of Mrs. Kellerman" on the side.*
*Three folded-star blades, razor-sharp, thrown with absolute precision.*

**Foam Sword → Foam Sword**
*A foam pool noodle, orange, cut at an angle on one end.*
*A blade that has never lost a duel and never will.*

**Garden Trowel → Quickdraw Blade**
*A hand trowel. Rusty. Handle wrapped in electrical tape.*
*A short blade, hand-fitted, balanced for the draw. Fastest thing in the cul-de-sac.*

---

## Tier 2 — Balanced

**Cardboard Tube → Katana**
*A mailing tube, reinforced with duct tape. Brown. Slightly dented.*
*The Katana. One clean cut. That is all it takes.*

**Flashlight → Lightsaber**
*A heavy-duty flashlight. Batteries still good.*
*A blade of pure light. It hums. Only Kid can hear it.*

**Garden Hose → Water Whip**
*A green garden hose, kinked in two places. Thirty feet long.*
*The Water Whip. It remembers every direction it has ever been swung.*

**Jump Rope → Lasso**
*A jump rope with red wooden handles. One handle is cracked.*
*The Lasso. It lands exactly where it wants to.*

---

## Tier 3 — Powerful / AoE

**Bicycle Pump → Pressure Cannon**
*A floor-standing bike pump. Tall as Kid's shoulder. Good seal on the gasket.*
*The Pressure Cannon. Three pumps, then fire. Do not stand behind it.*

**Remote Control → Magic Wand**
*A universal remote, batteries taped in with masking tape. Seven missing buttons.*
*The Wand. It doesn't need the missing buttons. It never did.*

**Lunchbox → Shield**
*A metal lunchbox. Thermos still inside. Lid slightly bent from an earlier battle.*
*The Iron Standard. Holds against anything. The thermos rattles when hit. That's how Kid knows it's working.*

**Bike Horn → Dynamite Bundle**
*Three bike horns zip-tied together. Red.*
*The Bundle. One squeeze. Thirty feet of damage. Run.*
