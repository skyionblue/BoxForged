# Milepost Marshal

**What it actually is:** A stop sign. Octagonal, red, weathered. One of the four on the cul-de-sac corners.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A broad-shouldered sheriff with an octagonal badge for a head, the word STOP embossed across it like a title. Arm-rods that end in flat blaster-faces. It plants its feet. It does not move until it decides to move. Then it moves fast.

## Lore

It used to mean something. Before. A Milepost Marshal doesn't tell you to stop because it's angry — it tells you to stop because it genuinely believes that's the correct thing. That's what makes it worse than angry. You can argue with angry. The Marshal doesn't argue. It reads the sign. The sign says stop. You haven't stopped. There's only one thing left to do about that.

## Audio

- "Stop." *(identical to Gnome Soldiers — by design. The Unimaginative have one word.)*
- A metallic clang on the flat body when hit — the sound of a street sign taking a knock
