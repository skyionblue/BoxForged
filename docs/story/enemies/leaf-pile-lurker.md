# Leaf Pile Lurker

**What it actually is:** A mound of dead, dry leaves. Brown. Autumn debris that nobody raked.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A hunched figure built entirely of compacted dead leaves, held together by something not quite visible. No face — just a hollow between layers where shadow sits.

## Lore

You'll hear it before you see it. A dry rustle, like something turning over in its sleep. Leaf Pile Lurkers aren't mean — they're just what happens when things get left alone too long. They're mostly sad. But sad things still bite. Watch the piles. Watch all of them.

## Audio

- A slow dry rustle building to a burst — like someone running a stick along a pile of paper
- No words. Not even close to words.
