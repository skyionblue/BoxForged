# SpinCycle — Boss

**What it actually is:** A front-loading washing machine. Drum interior, porthole window, stainless controls.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A drum-headed heavyweight brawler. The drum is its skull — dented, off-white, reinforced. The porthole window is the eye, fogged from inside. The controls are a chest panel, lights still blinking through grime. It shakes the ground when it walks.

## Lore

SpinCycle used to run three times a day. You could set a clock by it — if clocks still worked. Nobody knows when it stopped being just a machine, or whether it was ever just a machine. What's certain is that The Unimaginative stationed it here, at the end of the cul-de-sac, because it is exactly the kind of thing that ends attempts. It does not strategy. It does not negotiate. The drum turns. The spin cycle starts. The porthole eye fogs from the inside and something in there watches you and does not blink. When it is finished with you, it simply stops. Waits for the next load. Nobody has made it to the next room by going through SpinCycle. The kid is the first one to try it with a plan.

## Weapon — Suds Blob

SpinCycle doesn't fight alone. The drum vents laundry foam as a mid-fight attack — pale blue-white bubbles that pool across the arena floor and expand toward warmth. When struck, each blob divides: two smaller, slightly faster, angrier. The fight isn't just SpinCycle. It's everything SpinCycle leaves behind.

*What it actually is:* Laundry detergent foam forced through the drum seals under pressure.
*What the kid imagines it as:* Living soap creatures, iridescent and wobbly, doing their job.

**Audio:** A wet pressurized hiss as they vent from the drum; a high sharp pop when a blob splits.
