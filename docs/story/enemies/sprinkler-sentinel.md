# Sprinkler Sentinel

**What it actually is:** A garden pop-up sprinkler on a rotating head, buried flush with the lawn.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A rotating turret sentry — squat body flush with the ground, single glowing blue eye at the center of the spinning head, arms that are the spray nozzles themselves. It watches. Then it waters. The difference is intent.

## Lore

It was already in the ground before any of this started. That's the part that gets you. It was always there. The blue light is new. It doesn't blink. You'd almost feel watched, except it doesn't look at you quite long enough to prove it. Stay out of arcs. Move between them. If you freeze up, it'll find the pattern.

## Audio

- A mechanical click — the sound a sprinkler makes just before it fires — as a warning beat before each burst
- A low whir that rises in pitch when it locks on
