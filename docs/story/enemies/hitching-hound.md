# Hitching Hound

**What it actually is:** A coil of old braided rope, weather-stiffened. The kind left tied to a fence post.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A rope-body creature shaped like a large dog — four legs of thick braid, a head that's a knotted loop pulled tight, jaw formed by a fraying splice. It moves in short lunges. It wraps. It drags.

## Lore

It used to be tied to something. You can still see where the fraying starts — right at what would have been the collar end. It knows exactly how far it can go, and it goes exactly that far every time it lunges. The problem is that far is usually far enough. It doesn't bark. It just hits the end of the reach and snaps back. Hits, snaps. Hits, snaps. Until it finds a new anchor. The new anchor is you.

## Audio

- A taut-rope snap — the sound of tension releasing — on lunge attacks
- A low, fibrous scrape across pavement when it repositions

## Behavior Note

The HitchingHound paces a short chain radius, always taut — it wants to chase but can't find the slack. When the player enters range, it launches as far as its body allows, then recoils. Frustrated. Leashed. On its idle loop, the knot-head swings side to side, scanning. It does not look panicked. It looks like something that has done this a thousand times and will do it a thousand more.
