# Gnome Soldier

**What it actually is:** A ceramic garden gnome. Painted red hat, white beard, fixed smile.

**What the kid imagines it as:** A stocky armored soldier in terracotta plate mail, beard braided into a chin-guard, the painted smile now a grimace locked in place by an iron visor.

## Lore

They used to stand guard in Mrs. Hernandez's flower bed. There must have been four of them. Nobody knows what happened to the flower bed. If you see one alone, there's three more you haven't seen yet. They don't come one at a time. That's the whole trick of them.

## Audio

- "Stop." *(flat, procedural — the word of something enforcing a rule, not making a choice)*
- A short ceramic knock — knuckle on a clay pot — when they charge
