# The Ending

---

## Ending Narration

*The adult voice from the opening prologue returns. Quieter now. Like something has been resolved that couldn't quite be said before.*

---

There is a moment, after the mills fall quiet, that is very hard to describe.

Adults who were near the World Tree that day said the air changed first. Not cooler or warmer — just different. Like a window had opened somewhere far away. Some of them stopped what they were doing without knowing why. Some of them looked up.

What Kid did after the last gate opened is not agreed upon. Some said they climbed the tree. Some said they just sat at the base of it, in the roots, for a long time, with the box still on. What is agreed upon is what happened next.

The mills restarted. Not the way they had been running — not under grey management, not processing, not outputting. They ran the way a thing runs when someone who loves it is finally running it. The cardboard that came out was different. You could feel it. You could fold it and something happened at the crease — something that made you want to keep folding.

Within a week, boxes were moving again.

Within a month, a kid in an apartment building in a city two thousand miles away put a box on her head. She didn't know why. She stood in front of the bathroom mirror. She looked at herself.

Then she looked around her room and decided it was a castle.

Adults don't talk about this part. They talk about infrastructure. They talk about supply chains. They talk about what got restored and what is still broken.

But some of them, the ones who were there, still look up sometimes for no reason.

## Final Line

*The last thing the player reads before the credits.*

The box was always a door. Kid just found out where it led.
