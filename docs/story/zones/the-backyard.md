# Zone 1: The Backyard

**What it actually is:** A post-apocalyptic suburban backyard. Overgrown. Quiet. Apple tree, old fence, shed.

**What the kid sees:** A feudal Japanese training ground. Cherry blossom apple tree. Paper lanterns. Stone walls. Training dummies. The shed becomes a fortress. The fence becomes a castle wall.

---

## Zone Intro Narration

The box goes on and the backyard changes.

Not slowly. Not like a dream. It just — shifts. The dead apple tree catches a blossom — one pink blossom, high branch — and the fence stops leaning and the boards straighten and the shed, which has been a shed for thirty years, becomes something older. Stone walls. Paper lanterns unlit. Training dummies at the far end of a packed-dirt courtyard.

The grass is still dead underneath. He knows that. The broom handle is still a broom handle. Both things are true.

He grips the handle. He takes a breath. The courtyard stretches out quiet and enormous. Somewhere past the fence, The Unimaginative are moving through streets that look nothing like this.

Let them. He knows what he sees.
