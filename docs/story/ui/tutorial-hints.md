# Tutorial Hints

*Written in the Kid's voice — talking to himself, or maybe to the box.*

---

**Basic Movement**
"The whole street is open. I can go anywhere. Better figure out what's where first."

**Attack**
"If I swing right when they get close — yeah. That's it. That's the timing."

**Dodge**
"I can roll out of anything if I move early enough. Before it lands, not after."

**Parry**
"If I hold the block right when it hits — not before — something happens. I can feel the window."

**Counter (Parry Window)**
"There. Right after the parry. That's the moment. I have to take it."

**Weapon Pickup**
"Something on the ground. Could be anything. In this place, could be everything."

**Box / Class Switching**
"The Safe Zone. I can change the box here. Different box, different way of fighting. I should think about this."

**Taking Damage / Health**
"I'm still here. Keep moving. The box takes some of it."
